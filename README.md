# Spartan Agentic AI Studio: course material

This is the free course material of the Spartan Agentic AI Studio at Michigan State University. In the course you learn to direct a small team of AI agents: you give it a job too big to do by hand, it plans, works and checks its own work, and you step in where your judgment matters. You don't need any programming, and you don't need to be at MSU.

Course site, with the videos and the download: https://agentic-spartans.github.io/course/

Start here: watch video 1, then set up your computer with video 2. On Windows, the [Windows walkthrough](https://agentic-spartans.github.io/course/windows/) shows each step with a picture.

## What is here

- [AgentTeams](AgentTeams/): the agent teams used in the videos, one folder per video that has a team (`clip02` goes with video 2).
- [slides](slides/): the video slides as PDFs.
- [problems](problems/): problems from the Studio, each with a page that explains it and a team you can open in OpenCode.
- [references.md](references.md): short readings that go with the videos.

## Get the files

Download the course files from the course site and put the folder in your Studio folder, as video 1 shows. A newer download unzips beside the older one, so your own work is never overwritten.

To get everything in this repository, click the green **Code** button at the top of this page, then **Download ZIP**.

## Using it on your own

Everything here works without an MSU account. You pay for the AI yourself:

- **You bring your own key.** Make an account on openrouter.ai, buy credit, then make a key and give it a spending limit, so a mistake cannot cost more. Video 2 shows where the key goes.
- **Help for enrolled MSU students is in the D2L course. Enrolled and can't get into D2L? Email junlin@msu.edu, subject "Studio: access".** Open an issue in this repository if a file is broken.

What you type and the files the agents read go to OpenRouter and the model's maker. Use public or made-up material only.

Never share your key, and never put it in a file inside these folders.
