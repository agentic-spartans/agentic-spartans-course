# AgentTeams

The Spartan Agentic AI Studio course download: the teams used in the videos, in one folder per video that has a team (clip03 is video 3). Videos 4, 5 and 7 have no folder of their own: in video 7 the teams come from the problem bank, and for your own project you copy the starter team in clip08. Keep this folder in your Studio folder. Its name adds a version and a date, such as AgentTeams_V3_2026-11-09; the videos show it as AgentTeams. It is the same folder. To run a team, open the team's own folder in OpenCode (for example clip02 › clip02_01_simple-team), never AgentTeams or a video folder. Keep files you care about out of the original team folders, because the agents can change anything inside the folder they are opened in. Your own project is a copy of a team folder, made next to the original (for a first project, copy `clip08_01_starter` in clip08); keep a backup copy of its files elsewhere.

- `clip01`, Why a team: `clip01_01_tools` (an AI with no tools next to an agent with tools), then `clip01_02_one_agent` and `clip01_02_team` (one agent against a writer plus a checker, on the same task). `clip01_02_team` is the simple team of video 2, without its README.
- `clip02`, Setup, first run and the team files: `clip02_01_simple-team`, the simple team (a writer and a checker).
- `clip03`, The full team: `clip03_01_full-team`, an orchestrator and five roles (planner, implementer, reviewer, verifier, report writer).
- `clip06`, Tools: `clip06_01_skill` (one agent that loads a plant-care skill on its own), then `clip06_02_make-skill` (one agent with no skills, where you make your first one).
- `clip08`, Build your project: `clip08_01_starter`, the starter team to copy for your own project.
